CLENC CODES — KSh 3,500 Starter Business Website
================================================

Files:
- index.html: page content and sections
- style.css: colorful responsive design
- script.js: WhatsApp, address, hours, map and mobile menu settings

CUSTOMIZE BEFORE SHOWING A REAL CLIENT
1. Open script.js in VS Code.
2. Replace BUSINESS.whatsapp with the client's WhatsApp number in international format, digits only.
   Example: Kenyan 0712 345 678 becomes 254712345678.
3. Replace BUSINESS.address, BUSINESS.hours, BUSINESS.phoneDisplay and BUSINESS.mapQuery.
4. Replace the boutique name and text in index.html to match the client's business.
5. Replace image URLs with client-approved images if needed.
6. Open index.html in a browser and test all navigation links, the WhatsApp buttons, and map.
7. Search in index.html for "Nairobi, Kenya" and replace any remaining demo-only text as appropriate.

LOCAL TEST
Double-click index.html to open it in your browser. Internet is needed for Google Fonts, Unsplash photos, WhatsApp and Google Maps.

PUBLISH ON NETLIFY (FREE STATIC HOSTING)
1. Keep index.html, style.css and script.js together in this folder.
2. Visit https://app.netlify.com/drop and sign in/create a Netlify account.
3. Drag the project folder (or a ZIP of its contents, depending on the current interface) into the deployment area.
   If folder upload is not accepted, upload the three files via Netlify's supported manual deploy workflow.
4. Netlify will provide a URL such as https://your-site-name.netlify.app.
5. Open the published URL on both a phone and a computer and test every button.

PACKAGE SCOPE
This is a static business page template. Hosting and a Netlify subdomain are free on the suitable free plan, subject to platform terms. A custom domain, paid hosting, online payments, product inventory, database, and ongoing maintenance are not included in the KSh 3,500 starter offer.
